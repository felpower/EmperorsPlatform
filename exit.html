2. Effective quarter and signed declaration

Our quarters are Q1 February–April, Q2 May–July, Q3 August–October and Q4 November–January. Select the quarter start that is also written on your signed declaration. The current quarter remains available until and including the first day of its second month (Vienna time). For example, an exit effective 1 November can be submitted through 1 December; from 2 December this quarter is no longer available. The year refers to the year the quarter begins.

3. Contributions and returning to the team

Regular membership contributions remain due until your exit takes effect. Previously outstanding contributions remain payable afterwards. Submitting this form does not initiate a payment.

If you return within twelve months of the effective exit date, the regular membership contributions for the period between exit and return are generally payable retrospectively. The club body authorised under the statutes may grant a full or partial exemption for objective reasons, including voluntary work for the club. The decision and any amount due will be communicated in writing before rejoining. The applicable statutes and valid contribution rules govern this arrangement.

No website account required. The club checks the member assignment manually when a name is ambiguous. An administrative review does not change the recorded date of receipt.
