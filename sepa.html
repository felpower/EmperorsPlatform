3. Contribution collection schedule

Membership contributions are normally collected on the following dates:

  • Q1: February–April — 10 February
  • Q2: May–July — 10 May
  • Q3: August–October — 10 August
  • Q4: November–January — 10 November

If the date is not a bank business day, collection takes place on the next bank business day. The club will notify the account holder of the actual amount and collection date at least seven calendar days before each debit, including any changes to the usual schedule.

The club normally sends these notices via WhatsApp. If the account holder cannot receive them there, please contact p.felbauer@emperors.at to arrange another way to receive the notices.

If you use a parent's or another person's account, that account holder must read and accept the collection agreement below.

Submitting this form does not initiate a payment and does not create a website account. Existing bank details are only changed after Finance reviews the submission.
